Privacy policy

PySESM data sync. Effective 3 October 2026.

Who we are

PySESM data sync is the Google OAuth application of the SESM research group (Tecnológico de Costa Rica). Group members use it with rclone to copy the research data of the PySESM project between their computers and the project's folder in Google Drive. See the home page.

What it accesses

The application requests one permission: access to Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). rclone uses it only to list, upload, download and update the files of the project's data folder, and to transfer ownership of those files within the group when a member leaves the project.

What it collects

Nothing. The application has no server, database or analytics. rclone runs on the member's own computer and talks directly to Google; the access token Google issues is stored only in that member's rclone configuration file. The group's maintainers never receive members' tokens, files or Google account data through this application.

Sharing and use

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A member can revoke the application's access at any time on the third-party connections page of their Google account, and delete the token by removing the remote from their rclone configuration. Files they copied to the project folder remain there under the group's control.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/SESM-TEC/pysesm-oauth/issues, or write to Pablo Alvarado Moya, palvarado [at] tec [dot] ac [dot] cr.
