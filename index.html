PySESM data sync

SESM research group, Tecnológico de Costa Rica

PySESM data sync is the Google OAuth application that members of the SESM research group use to copy the research data of the PySESM project between their own computers and the project's folder in Google Drive. The copying is done by rclone, an open-source program that runs on each member's computer.

It is not a public service: there is nothing to sign up for, no server, and no website besides these two pages. Only group members, who receive setup instructions from the group, use it.

What it can access

When a member connects rclone, Google asks them to grant access to their Google Drive. rclone uses that access only to read and write the files of the project's data folder. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why Google shows a warning

The application is used by a small research group and has not gone through Google's verification, which is meant for apps offered to the public. Google therefore shows the message "Google hasn't verified this app" the first time a member connects. Members who expect it may continue; anyone else should not.
